Privacy Policy for Noob Topup

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Noob Topup mobile application and website (accessible at https://noobtopup.com/). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and digital game top-up services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information to provide and improve our game diamond and voucher delivery services:

  • Account Information: When you create an account or sign in to purchase gaming items, we collect necessary credentials such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Game Identifiers: Your in-game Player ID (UID) and Server ID to process in-game top-up orders accurately.
  • Device and Usage Information: Device tokens for sending push notifications regarding order status, discounts, and flash sales.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your game top-up orders.
  • To send transaction updates, delivery confirmations, and customer support messages.
  • To deliver relevant promotional notifications and special offers via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • To maintain app security and prevent fraudulent transactions.

3. Push Notifications & Permissions

Our app requests notification permissions (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+) to inform you of:

  • Instant order delivery status.
  • Special diamond price discounts and weekly/monthly membership offers.

You can enable or disable push notifications at any time directly through your device's Android System Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers that assist us in operating our platform:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used for secure push notification delivery and app infrastructure.
  • Authorized Payment Gateways: Payments are handled securely via authorized merchant payment gateways. Noob Topup does not store sensitive credit/debit card numbers or mobile banking PINs.

5. Data Security

All communication between the Noob Topup application and our servers is strictly encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption protocols. We take all reasonable measures to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to your personal data or request deletion of your account and associated records. To request account deletion or data removal, please contact our support team at support@noobtopup.com or message us via our official Telegram support channel.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for general audiences who play mobile games. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental guidance.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Website: https://noobtopup.com/
  • Official App: Noob Topup (com.noobtopup.app)
  • Email: support@noobtopup.com
