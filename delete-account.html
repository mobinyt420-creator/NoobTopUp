Noob Topup - Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Noob Topup (com.noobtopup.app) | Service URL: https://noobtopup.com/

At Noob Topup, we respect your privacy and provide you with complete control over your personal account and data. In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy, users can request the deletion of their account and associated data without needing to retain the app installed.

1. What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon receiving your confirmed deletion request, we will permanently delete:

  • Your account profile (Name, registered Phone number, Email address).
  • Saved in-game Player IDs (UIDs) and server information.
  • Push notification device tokens and messaging subscriptions.
  • Login session records and personal account settings.

2. What Data May Be Retained?

Certain historical transaction records (such as payment reference IDs, amounts, and dates) may be retained strictly for financial accounting, tax compliance, and fraud prevention as required by applicable laws. These records are securely archived and not used for marketing or profiling.

3. How Long Does Deletion Take?

Account deletion requests are processed by our administrative team within 24 to 48 hours. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered.
